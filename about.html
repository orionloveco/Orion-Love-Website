The Profile

Born in Delta
In the valley since 2010

Rooted on the
Western Slope.

I was born in Delta and spent my early years in Colorado before my family moved around the country, from the East Coast to the West Coast. In 2010 we came back to the valley, and I’ve been here since. After living in a lot of places, I can say the landscape here is one of a kind, and I don’t take it for granted.

Before real estate, I spent almost ten years building custom cabinetry. That work teaches you to take pride in quality and to notice the details most people walk past. I bring the same habits to selling a home: careful preparation, attention to how a house presents, and doing things properly rather than quickly.

I focus on sellers for a personal reason too. I have kids, and working with sellers lets me build my schedule around the time I get with them. Having moved so often growing up, I also know how much a move asks of a family.

What I want to give every seller is a clear path.

That means knowing where the home stands, what is worth doing before it goes live, and what happens at each step through closing. You do not have to be ready to list to start that conversation.

My work is centered on Grand Junction and Mesa County, including Redlands, Orchard Mesa, Fruita, Palisade, Clifton, and the surrounding areas. For sellers who want to go deeper, the site includes a clearer look at my seller approach, a local home value review, and the Mesa County area guide.

And yes, the moustache is real. It’s usually how people remember me.

A final read before you list.

If selling is on the table, the first conversation should make the decision clearer: where the home fits, what matters before launch, and whether the timing supports the move.
