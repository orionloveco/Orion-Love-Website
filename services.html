Seller Method Overview

A Four-Stage Sequence
From Price to Close

Every sale is different, but the method is consistent: set the right number, prepare for buyer response, launch with intent, then protect terms through negotiation and execution.

01

Price

Set an explainable list strategy using current competition, recent closings, and neighborhood behavior.

02

Prepare

Prioritize repairs, presentation, and pre-list decisions that influence perceived value and offer quality.

03

Launch

Go live with strong media, complete MLS positioning, and coordinated exposure to buyers and agents.

04

Negotiate & Close

Evaluate offers, negotiate terms, and manage inspection-to-closing details so timelines and leverage hold.

Stage Deep Dives

How the Method Works in Practice

Instead of a generic services menu, this is the operating detail behind each stage of the sale.

Stage 1 · Price

Set the Number with Context, Not Guesswork

Pricing is built from real market signals in your submarket so you can justify your position from day one.

  • Comparable sales and active competition review
  • Positioning strategy by timing and inventory pressure
  • Pre-planned adjustment thresholds if early feedback shifts

Comp-Led Pricing

Recent closings and active alternatives define the initial range.

Neighborhood Specific

Redlands and Orchard Mesa are not priced with the same buyer assumptions.

Stage 2 · Prepare

Prep That Protects Value

We focus on high-impact updates, showing flow, and presentation details buyers actually react to.

  • Room-by-room prep priorities
  • Optional pre-list inspection strategy
  • Vendor coordination for speed
Stage 3 · Launch

Market Entry with Intent

Your launch combines media quality, listing clarity, and distribution so first impressions convert to showings.

  • Professional photo/video package
  • MLS + portal + agent-network distribution
  • Digital promotion aligned to buyer segments
Stage 4 · Negotiate / Manage to Close

Protect Terms and Keep the Deal Moving

Offer strength, contingency risk, inspection issues, appraisal pressure, and deadlines are managed as one system.

01

Offer Evaluation

We compare net, contingencies, financing, and closing timelines before selecting a path.

02

Negotiation Strategy

Counter terms are built to protect leverage while keeping qualified buyers engaged.

03

Contract-to-Close Execution

Inspection, appraisal, title, and deadline coordination stay visible so late surprises are reduced.
