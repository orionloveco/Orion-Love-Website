Property Preparation

Most obstacles in a home sale are preventable. We address them before buyers find them, focusing on preparation that genuinely improves the way your home shows and supports the number you want the market to accept.

Staging & Presentation

A walk-through with clear, practical direction on what helps, what hurts, and what buyers in your price range are most likely to notice the minute they walk in.

Optional but often worthwhile. Identifying issues before listing means you negotiate from strength instead of scrambling when a buyer's inspection brings up problems later.
