Comparative Market Analysis

Monthly Market Updates

Strategic Positioning

03

Property Preparation

Most issues are preventable. We address them before buyers find them — focusing only on changes that genuinely impact buyer perception and value, not unnecessary fixes that eat into your return.

Staging Consultation

I walk through your home and provide clear, practical guidance on what helps and what hurts. Simple, targeted adjustments make a measurable difference to buyers.

Pre-Listing Inspection

Optional but often worth it. Finding issues early means negotiating from a position of strength — not scrambling to respond during buyer inspections.

Vendor Coordination

Need repairs, cleaning, or touch-ups? I coordinate trusted local contractors to get work done quickly and correctly — without you managing the logistics.

04

Clear Communication

You'll never wonder what's happening with your home. I keep you informed at every step — with weekly updates, honest showing feedback, and direct answers whenever you need them.

Weekly Updates

Every week, you receive a summary of showing activity, buyer feedback, market changes, and recommended next steps. No silence. No chasing me down.

Honest Showing Feedback

After showings, I share what buyers actually said — the good and the bad. If there's an issue, we address it. If pricing needs adjustment, I'll tell you directly.

Direct Access

Call, text, or email. I respond to all inquiries within 24 hours — usually faster. You won't be bounced to an assistant or left wondering.

05

Negotiation & Closing

Getting an offer is one thing. Getting to closing — on your terms — is another. I manage every detail from offer through keys, so nothing falls through and there are no surprises at the closing table.

Buyer Qualification

Before presenting an offer, I verify buyer financing and review their track record. No wasting time on weak offers that won't make it to the finish line.

Contract Review

We review all terms, conditions, contingencies, and timelines together. You'll understand exactly what you're agreeing to — and what's negotiable.

Transaction Management

I coordinate inspection, appraisal, title, and closing. You'll know what's happening and when. No loose threads. No surprises.
