Seller Method Spine

A Four-Stage Operating System
for a Controlled Sale

Every listing is unique, but the method is consistent: set a defensible number, prepare for buyer response, launch with intent, then protect terms through close.

Stage 1

Price

Goal

Set an explainable opening strategy backed by current competition, recent closings, and neighborhood behavior.

Key Decisions

  • Where your defensible range starts based on active alternatives.
  • How your submarket timing changes initial positioning.
  • What adjustment thresholds trigger action if early feedback shifts.

What Orion Is Doing

Building a comp-led range, stress-testing market assumptions, and documenting the first two adjustment paths before launch.

What You Should Expect

A clear pricing rationale you can explain to anyone and a plan that avoids reactive price drops.

Stage 2

Prepare

Goal

Improve perceived value and offer quality by focusing effort on changes buyers actually react to.

Key Decisions

  • Which repairs and cosmetic updates move the needle most.
  • Whether a pre-list inspection reduces risk in your situation.
  • How staging and room flow should be prioritized by buyer profile.

What Orion Is Doing

Setting prep priorities, coordinating trusted vendors when needed, and keeping timeline and spend tied to expected return.

What You Should Expect

A practical prep plan, not an endless to-do list, with clear tradeoffs between speed, cost, and impact.

Stage 3

Launch

Goal

Enter the market with complete positioning so early attention converts into qualified showings and serious offers.

Key Decisions

  • How listing media and remarks frame value before first showings.
  • Which channels and audiences need priority in week one.
  • What showing feedback patterns count as signal, not noise.

What Orion Is Doing

Coordinating media, controlling MLS accuracy, and executing a launch sequence across portal, agent-network, and digital channels.

What You Should Expect

Visible launch momentum, clear weekly performance context, and quick interpretation of buyer response.

Stage 4

Negotiate & Close

Goal

Protect your net and timeline by treating offers, contingencies, inspections, appraisal, and deadlines as one system.

Key Decisions

  • Which offer path is strongest when net and certainty conflict.
  • How to counter while preserving leverage and buyer engagement.
  • When to hold firm versus trade terms to protect close probability.

What Orion Is Doing

Comparing offer quality beyond headline price, leading negotiation terms, and managing every contract milestone through closing.

What You Should Expect

Transparent guidance, rapid escalation on issues, and no silent handoff once you are under contract.

Decision Gates

Adaptability Is Built In,
Not Improvised

The method includes predefined trigger points so adjustments happen on evidence, not stress.

If showing activity is high but offers are weak

We adjust terms framing and buyer qualification filters before changing price.

If feedback repeats the same objection

We treat it as market signal, then adjust presentation, positioning, or concessions by priority.

If inspection requests threaten momentum

We re-rank requests by net impact and negotiate from risk, timeline, and replacement cost.

If appraisal pressure appears

We assemble comp support and negotiate options that protect your position and closing path.

Communication Protocol

How the Method Stays Governed
from List to Close

Communication is part of execution. You get a defined rhythm, clear escalation rules, and direct access when a real decision appears.

Weekly operating update

Showing velocity, buyer patterns, and market movement tied to your current stage decisions.

Signal vs noise filter

Trigger-based changes

Pricing, prep, exposure, and negotiation shifts follow pre-agreed thresholds.

Direct escalation access
