Accurate Market Analysis

Comparative Market Analysis

We review active competition and recent closings in your specific Grand Junction neighborhood — Redlands, Fruita, Palisade, Orchard Mesa — so pricing reflects what buyers here are actually choosing.

Monthly Market Bearings

Every 30 days, you receive updated data on Mesa County new listings, closed sales, and price trends — so your strategy stays grounded in what's happening now.

Strategic Positioning

We discuss timing, initial price point, and adjustment strategy. The goal is to attract serious Grand Junction buyers early — protecting your leverage, not chasing a fast close at the wrong number.

03

Property Preparation

Most obstacles in a Grand Junction home sale are preventable. We address them before buyers find them — focusing only on preparation that genuinely moves the needle, nothing that doesn't earn its cost.

Staging & Presentation

A walk-through with clear, practical direction — what helps, what hurts, and what Grand Junction buyers in your price range respond to most.

Pre-Listing Inspection

Optional but often worth it. Identifying issues before listing means you negotiate from strength — not scrambling when a buyer's inspector finds something first.

Vendor Coordination

Repairs, cleaning, touch-ups — I connect you with trusted Grand Junction contractors who deliver quality work on a listing timeline, without you managing the logistics.

04

Clear Communication

You'll never be left wondering. Every stage of your sale comes with clear direction, honest showing feedback, and direct communication — without you having to chase it.

Weekly Updates

Every week: showing activity, buyer feedback, Mesa County market changes, and a clear next step. No silence. No guessing. No chasing me down.

Honest Showing Feedback

After every showing, I share what buyers actually said — the good and the critical. If something needs to change, I tell you directly and we course-correct early.

Direct Access

Call, text, or email. I respond to all inquiries within 24 hours — usually faster. You won't be bounced to an assistant or left wondering.

05

Negotiation & Closing

Receiving an offer is one step. Closing on your terms is the goal. I manage every detail from offer through keys so nothing falls through and there are no surprises at the table.

Buyer Qualification

Before presenting any offer, I verify buyer financing and assess their track record. No wasted time on offers that won't survive the route to closing.

Contract Review

We review all terms, conditions, contingencies, and timelines together. You'll understand exactly what you're agreeing to — and what's negotiable.

Transaction Management

I coordinate inspection, appraisal, title, and closing. You'll know what's happening and when. No loose threads. No surprises.
