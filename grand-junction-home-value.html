What Drives Value

Why No Two Grand Junction
Homes Are Worth the Same

Online estimates are built from public records. They do not know your updates, your lot, your views, your competition, or how buyers are responding right now. A real market analysis does.

Neighborhood & Submarket

The Redlands, Orchard Mesa, downtown Grand Junction, Fruita, and Palisade do not trade the same. Hyper-local comps matter more than broad averages.

Recent Comparable Sales

The most useful signal is what actually closed recently near your property, not what someone hopes to get and not what a portal guesses.

Condition & Updates

Kitchens, baths, flooring, roof age, HVAC, paint, and deferred maintenance all affect what buyers are willing to pay.

Views & Lot Features

Monument views, lot size, RV parking, irrigation, privacy, and usable outdoor space can create meaningful premiums in this market.

Active Competition

Your home is priced in relation to what else buyers can choose today. That is why active competition matters just as much as past sales.

Timing & Inventory

Inventory levels and buyer urgency change throughout the year. A smart pricing strategy responds to current conditions, not old momentum.
