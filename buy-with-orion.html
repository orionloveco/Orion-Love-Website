From Loma to Palisade, the parts of this valley feel very different to live in. I help buyers work out which area fits their commute, budget and plans before they start touring houses.

Local search context

Don’t narrow the map too early.

If you’re weighing Fruita against the Redlands, or Palisade against Orchard Mesa, look at both before you rule one out. A home you’d skip in one area can be a good buy in the other.
