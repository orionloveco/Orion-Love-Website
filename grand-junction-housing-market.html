Mesa County · Market Numbers

The Grand Junction housing market.

What homes are selling for across Grand Junction and Mesa County, how long they take, and how much sellers are giving up to close.

Updated every MondayMonth ending October 11, 2026

Mesa County right now.

In the past month, the median sold price in Mesa County was $418,500, up about 5% from $400,000 in the same period last year. Homes sold after a median of 70 days, for 99.1% of their final list price, at a median of $272 per square foot.

It depends on your price range.

Homes priced from $400,000 to $500,000 are moving fastest, with 3.8 months of supply. Homes priced at $750,000 and up have 7.8 months, so buyers there have more room to negotiate.

Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every home now for sale at the recent pace of sales. Around five to six months is commonly treated as a balanced market.

Area by area.

Over the past three months, Loma and Mack had the highest median sold price of the twelve areas ($730,000) and Clifton the lowest ($325,250). North Grand Junction had the most sales in the past month (32) and the most homes for sale (176).

Each area’s median sold price covers the past three months, so a small area isn’t judged on a handful of sales.

Nearly half of sales include a seller concession.

In the past month, 87 of 192 sales (45%) included money from the seller toward the buyer’s costs. The median concession was $7,000. How to weigh a concession against the price.

98 listings cut their price in one week.

That was the week ending October 11. The median cut was 3.7% off the original list price.

About a third of listings have been for sale more than 90 days.

On October 13, 35.9% of the homes for sale in Mesa County had been listed for more than 90 days. Most price cuts trace back to the first price, which is why a local home value review comes before the listing.

These are closed sales of single-family homes, condos and townhomes in Mesa County. Land is left out. County figures cover the past month, counted as four full weeks, because a single week has too few sales to trust. Homes for sale were counted on October 13, 2026.

Based on information from the Grand Junction Area REALTOR® Association MLS for the period July 13, 2026 through October 11, 2026.
