Local Context

Why location changes the plan.

Where your home sits changes who shows up to see it.

Buyers don’t judge every home in the county by the same standard. Someone shopping in Palisade and someone shopping in Clifton usually want different things, and they size up homes differently.

01
Setting

A view, a quiet street or a bit of land can change what buyers think a home is worth.

02
Comparison

Buyers weigh your home against what else they could buy nearby, not against a county average.

03
Condition

How much repairs matter depends on the buyer, and some areas are far more budget-sensitive than others.

04
Timing

How many homes are for sale, and how quickly they sell, varies a lot from one area to the next.

Recent sales show where prices have been. The homes buyers can choose from today show where yours will land.
