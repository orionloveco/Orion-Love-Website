## `areas/` and `valley/`: Canva Pro stock photos

Licensed through Orion's Canva Pro account. **Canva's license allows web use only up to 480,000 pixels per image**, so these are stored at 848 × 477 (≈404,000 px). Never upscale or replace them with larger versions; crop only. `areas/` holds the area guides' Market Read photos (848 × 477, shown about 520px wide). `valley/` holds hero backgrounds (848 × 565 = 479,120 px, just under the limit) used behind navy gradients on the area heroes, Sellers, Buyers, Home Value and Areas pages; the tint hides their softness. They are too small to show full-width without a tint. One photo per area; confirm the location with Orion before labeling a photo with a place.
